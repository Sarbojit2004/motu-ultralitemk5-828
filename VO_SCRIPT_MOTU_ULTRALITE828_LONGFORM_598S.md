# Voiceover script — MOTU UltraLite-mk5 & MOTU 828
## Long-form · 598 seconds · 1920×1080 · 30 fps

**Language:** English only.

**Tone (Gemini brief Stage 9):** a deliberate blend of two of the three proposed
directions, held identical across all three deliverables so the set reads as one
family. **The Technical Authority** is the spine — this audience respects
verifiable facts over hyperbole, and the whole narrative rests on shared verified
specifications. **The Operational Pragmatist** carries the Problem (chapter 1) and
Transformation (chapter 5) passages, which are about workflow friction rather than
engineering. *The Creative Catalyst* is deliberately not used: its rising urgency
works against the quiet confidence the other two establish.

**Delivery:** paced, articulate, no radio-announcer excitement. Numbers are read
in full rather than as digits. Leave the marked holds silent — the picture is
carrying those beats.

**Sync:** every timestamp below is the true in-point of the beat it accompanies,
generated from the same `schedule.json` that renders the video, then re-verified
against the finished render.

**No burned-in captions.** A silent voiceover slot of exactly 598.000 s ships at
`public/vo/voiceover-longform.mp3` for the recorded read to drop into.
