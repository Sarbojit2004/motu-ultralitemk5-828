## Production notes

- The reel carries **no burned-in captions** — nothing spoken appears as on-screen text by design. On-screen typography is limited to product names, specifications, branding and contact details.
- Music bed sits at roughly −30 dBFS RMS with peaks near −9 dBFS, leaving clear headroom. Drop the narration in at around −16 to −14 dBFS RMS and it will sit on top without any ducking automation.
- SFX are already mixed low (0.18–0.5 gain) and are concentrated on cuts, so they punctuate rather than compete with speech.
- If the recorded read runs long, the natural trims are the second sentence of **[0:20]** and the second sentence of **[1:46]** — both are supporting detail rather than load-bearing claims.
